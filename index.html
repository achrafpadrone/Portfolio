About

How I got here

I grew up around my family's construction supply business, M2C Maroc, and I've been part of it since 2020, mostly the unglamorous side: chasing suppliers, checking deliveries landed on time, keeping the paperwork straight so nobody is guessing later. It taught me that most projects do not fail because of one big mistake. They fail because ten small things slip at once.

That is roughly why I am doing an MSc in Project Management now, at Liverpool John Moores University. My dissertation looks at how artificial intelligence is being used in construction, which is a good excuse to think seriously about where this industry is heading rather than just where it has been.

Outside of coursework I led the Moroccan Global Society at Huddersfield for two years, ran events for over 100 students, and somehow ended up rebuilding the society's entire social presence from nothing, which won us the Social Media Award. Different world to a construction site, same job really: plan it, coordinate the people, adjust when it goes wrong.
